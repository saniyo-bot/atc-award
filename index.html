Global Excellence Recognised: Technovalley Wins EC-Council ATC Circle of Excellence Award 2026

From over 3,200 Authorised Training Centres across 171 countries, only 27 have earned this prestigious global recognition. Technovalley Software India Pvt. Ltd., proudly representing Kerala, India, is among the world's Top 27 recipients of the EC-Council Global ATC Circle of Excellence Award 2026.

This prestigious recognition celebrates Technovalley excellence in cybersecurity education, professional training delivery, industry-oriented learning and commitment to developing globally skilled cybersecurity professionals.

A Global Recognition of Excellence

With more than 3,200 EC-Council Authorised Training Centres worldwide, Technovalley Software India Private Limited is proud to be among the Top 27 institutions selected for the prestigious ATC Circle of Excellence Award 2026. This global recognition marks a significant milestone in our 10-year journey of delivering internationally recognised cybersecurity education, professional certifications and industry-focused technology training.

Our decade-long partnership with EC-Council, a globally recognised cybersecurity certification organisation with a presence in more than 170 countries and a community of over 400,000 certified professionals, reflects our shared commitment to advancing cybersecurity education and professional development. Through this collaboration, we empower aspiring professionals with practical skills and industry-relevant expertise to address evolving cyber threats.

This prestigious award celebrates the collective efforts of our students, alumni, trainers, employees and global partners. It further strengthens our commitment to bridging the cybersecurity skills gap, developing industry-ready professionals and connecting Indian talent with global career opportunities.
